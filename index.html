Innovations

Newer technologies for modern fabrics

FR for Yarn Dyeing

Daiwa Chemical Industries, Japan offers a high concentration flame retardant for yarn dyeing, applied by the exhaust method in the dye bath for a durable FR effect.

PU Films for Lamination

Adhesive coated PU films for waterproof yet breathable laminated fabrics used in jackets and garments, in place of solvent based alternatives.

PU Coatings on Denim

Polyurethane resin coatings for denim and bottoms, giving textures such as a waxy, slippery or soft feel.

Natural Eco Finishes

Plant and animal derived extracts such as aloe, chitosan, squalene and herbs, offering properties like moisture retention and anti‑inflammatory effects. Suitable for organic cotton.
